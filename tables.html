Tables

<table>, <tr>, <th>

<table> creates a table, <tr> a row and <th> a header cell.

Attribute Role
border Border width of the table. 
<table><tr><th>Name</th><th>Age</th></tr></table>
Name Age

<td>

A normal data cell inside a row.

Attribute Role
colspan Number of columns the cell covers.
rowspan Number of rows the cell covers. 
<table>
  <tr><td>Ali</td><td>20</td></tr>
  <tr><td colspan="2">Total: 1 student</td></tr>
</table>
Ali 20
Total: 1 student
